doomgeneric, vendored for the kitchen computer
==============================================

Source:  https://github.com/ozkl/doomgeneric

doomgeneric is ozkl's portable fork of Chocolate Doom, which is based on
the DOOM source code released by id Software in 1997.

Only the files the kitchen computer builds are kept: the engine, its
headers and the stdio WAD reader. The platform backends (SDL, X11, Win32,
Allegro, emscripten, ...), the sound backends and the Makefiles are left
out; firmware/doom/dg_kitchen.c is the platform layer for this machine and
firmware/doom/build.sh builds it.

Local changes are marked with "kitchen:" comments.
